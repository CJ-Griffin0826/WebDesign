# One Piece

## Eiichiro Oda

While most likley not my favorite book, I like the idea that one piece presents to its readers. It spreads anti-government messaging along with a sense of togetherness for the working class. While a majority of the characters are pirates, we see that they are helping people across the seas be liberated from their tyranical rulers. Overall, I think the series is a good entry point for anyone who doesn't currently hold their government responsible for what they do.
